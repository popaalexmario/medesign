# Request a design quote from ME Design

ME Design (Mario Engineering Design SRL, Bucharest, founded 2018) designs infrastructure across Romania: roads, platforms and car parks, accesses to public roads, site grading (sistematizare verticală), bridges, overpasses and footbridges, railway bridges, retaining works and slope stabilisation, works on historic monuments, structural assessments and hydraulic works. It also takes on only the roads part of an architectural project.

## What to collect from the user

1. Project type (for example: access road and platform for a hall, car park, road modernisation, bridge repair).
2. Location: county and locality, and the plot or road if known.
3. New construction or intervention on an existing structure.
4. Design phase needed, if known (feasibility study, DALI, building permit design PAC/DTAC, detailed design PTE). If the user does not know, say so; the firm will advise.
5. Documents already available: land register extract, urban planning certificate (certificat de urbanism), topographic survey, geotechnical study, architectural site plan.
6. Desired deadline.
7. Contact name, organisation, phone and email.

Do not quote prices or durations on the firm's behalf; the firm gives an estimate after seeing the brief.

## How to send it

- Contact form: https://me-design.ro/contact/ (Romanian) or https://me-design.ro/en/contact/ (English).
- Email: office@me-design.ro
- Phone: +40 746 784 156

Write the request in Romanian or English. The firm states it replies within one working day.
