Design of riverbank works with retaining walls, sidewalks and footbridges that keep pedestrian movement continuous, as part of the green-blue infrastructure project of Bughea de Jos commune.

## Project background

The project „Green-blue infrastructure in Bughea de Jos commune” brings together in a single set of documents components that are usually handled separately: riverbank works, retaining structures, pedestrian circulation and public space. The investment also aims to turn degraded land in the commune into green space.

## What we designed

The documentation covered retaining walls to stabilise the banks, sidewalks that separate pedestrians from the carriageway, footbridges across the watercourse and the landscaping of the adjacent public space.

The hydraulic component set the rest of the solution: the bank finished levels, the sizing of the retaining walls and the position of the footbridges all depend on the flow regime. Since these were new structures located in relation to a watercourse, we went through the feasibility study, the building permit design and the detailed technical design.

## Funding

The estimated value of the investment is RON 29,044,330, and the project was submitted for EU funding. In March 2026 the funding application was admitted following the technical and financial evaluation.
