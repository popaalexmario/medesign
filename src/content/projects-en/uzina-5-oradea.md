Building permit design (DTAC) and detailed technical design (PTE) for the rehabilitation of the Uzina 5 drinking water treatment plant in Oradea, under the investment "BH-CL-1 Reabilitare Uzina 5 Oradea".

## Project context

Uzina 5 is the drinking water treatment plant in the south-east of Oradea. The 29,267 m² site lies between Victor Papilian Street and the Crișul Repede river, with enrichment basin no. 1 to the west. The client is Compania de Apă Oradea S.A., the city's water utility.

Uzina 5 is the newest of the city's water plants and treats water taken directly from the Crișul Repede river, through settling tanks and rapid filters. After rehabilitation it will have a new pre-oxidation station, all installations rehabilitated and a water quality laboratory with new equipment. The plant will be able to supply the whole city when needed, allowing the utility to take Uzina 4, Oradea's main water source, out of service for maintenance.

## What we designed

The works are carried out within the existing site, which is extended by 5,524 m² so that the minimum sanitary protection distances required by law are met. We prepared the documentation at the DTAC and PTE stages.

The internal circulation site plan includes a new 2,775 m² platform, roads and hardstandings along three alignments, footways linking the buildings, and earth soakaway ditches of 405 m and 167 m for stormwater. Within the site, existing structures of the plant are rehabilitated, including the rapid filters, the pipe gallery and the filtered water tanks, and new above-ground and underground structures are built.

## Funding

The rehabilitation of Uzina 5 is part of the regional water and wastewater infrastructure project for Bihor County, run by Compania de Apă Oradea under the Large Infrastructure Operational Programme (POIM) and phased beyond 2020. The total value of the regional project is RON 224 million, of which RON 125 million is EU grant funding. Almost RON 40 million is allocated to the works at Uzina 5.
