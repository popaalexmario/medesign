Rehabilitation design for a 64.00 m parabolic through-truss road bridge built in 1902.

## Project context

The Iron Bridge over the Timiș River in Lugoj Municipality was built in **1902**, as a parabolic truss girder with a **64.00 m span**, and is listed as a historic monument.

## Working on historic steel structures

A steel structure more than a hundred years old raises problems a modern bridge does not have:

- **The material** does not match the steel grades standardised today; its properties are established by testing, not by looking up a standard
- **Riveted connections** behave differently from welded or high-strength bolted connections
- **Fatigue** accumulated over more than a century of service has to be assessed explicitly
- **The original drawings** either no longer exist or no longer match the structure on site, which has been altered by successive interventions

## Heritage constraints

On a listed structure, the intervention cannot be chosen on technical grounds alone. Original elements must be kept as far as their condition allows, and replacements must be compatible with the historic make-up of the bridge. The documentation goes through an additional approval process.

## Documentation prepared

For Lugoj Municipality, we went through the intervention approval documentation (DALI), building permit design (DTAC) and detailed technical design (PTE) phases, starting from a measured survey of the actual structure and from the structural assessment.

## What it brings to the community

A bridge from 1902, listed as a historic monument, matters to Lugoj in two ways:

- **Keeping the crossing** over the Timiș River open, in a town where the alternatives mean significant detours
- **Preserving part of the town's identity**: the structure has been part of how Lugoj looks for more than a century
- **User safety**, by bringing the structure back to verified service parameters
- **Avoiding the cost of a new bridge**, significantly higher than rehabilitating a structure that can be saved

## Improvements on site

The intervention brings the 64.00 m parabolic truss girder back to a service condition verified by calculation, with the deteriorated elements treated and the original ones kept where their condition allows. Rehabilitating a historic structure means extending the life of an existing construction rather than replacing it, which is the option with the least impact on the town.
