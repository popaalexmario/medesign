Rehabilitation of the Iron Bridge in Lugoj, a road bridge with a 64.00 m parabolic truss girder built in 1902. The bridge reopened to traffic in November 2026.

## Project context

The Iron Bridge crosses the Timiș River in Lugoj Municipality. The structure has a parabolic truss girder with a **64.00 m span**, dates from 1902 and is listed as a historic monument. On a listed structure, original elements are kept as far as their condition allows, and the documentation goes through an additional approval process.

## What we designed

We prepared the intervention approval documentation (DALI), building permit design (DTAC) and detailed technical design (PTE) for Lugoj Municipality. The starting point was a measured survey of the structure as it stands and the structural assessment. The intervention brings the girder back to a service condition verified by calculation, with deteriorated elements treated and original ones kept where possible.

## Funding

The rehabilitation has approved funding of about RON 17 million through the „Anghel Saligny” National Investment Programme. By Local Council Decision no. 77/2026, Lugoj approved paying from the local budget 20% of the amount expected to be settled in 2026.

## Status

On 28 August 2026 the steel structure and reinforcement were partially accepted, with the designer, the site supervisor and the client present. The concrete deck slab of the carriageway was cast the next day. The bridge reopened to traffic and pedestrians on 3 November 2026. Lighting, some finishes and work on the four towers continue without closing the bridge.
