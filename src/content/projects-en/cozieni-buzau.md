Design for the upgrading of 13 local and commune roads in Cozieni commune, totalling 10.35 km, in the Buzău Subcarpathians.

## Project background

Cozieni lies in the Buzău Subcarpathians, an area of rugged relief with slopes prone to instability. Access to the households depends directly on the condition of the local roads. Here the main problem of the roads is not the pavement structure itself, but water: on slopes, surface water picks up speed, gets onto the carriageway, penetrates the structure and carries material away from the embankment slopes.

## What we designed

We prepared the intervention approval documentation (DALI) for 13 existing roads, 10.35 km in total. The network was analysed as a system: the collection points and discharge routes were fixed before the pavement structures were sized, because water discharged from one section becomes the problem of the section downstream. The drainage collection and discharge system is sized for the network as a whole, with controlled discharge points.

## Funding

The 13 roads include DC 60, the Cozieni – Punga – Pănătău commune road, on the section within Cozieni commune, from Pietrosu village (junction with county road DJ 203L) to the boundary with Pănătău commune. For this section, Cozieni obtained funding of about RON 11 million through the „Anghel Saligny” National Investment Programme. The 5.7 km section in Pănătău commune, between Plăișoru and Punga villages, was funded separately, through the National Investment Company (CNI), and has been built. Together, the two sections connect the area to national road DN10 in the Buzău Valley, within the „Ținutul Buzăului” UNESCO Geopark.
