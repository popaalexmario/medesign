Design of 13 local and commune roads totalling 10.35 km.

## Project background

For Cozieni commune, in Buzău County, we designed the upgrading of **13 local and commune roads**, totalling **10.35 km**.

## The terrain challenge

Cozieni lies in the Buzău Subcarpathians, an area of rugged relief with slopes prone to instability. In this setting the main problem of local roads is not the pavement structure itself, but **water**.

On slopes, surface water picks up speed and erosive energy. Without properly sized ditches, without culverts at every valley and without controlled discharge points, water gets onto the carriageway, penetrates the structure and carries material away from the embankment slopes.

## A network approach

Thirteen roads across varied terrain cannot be treated as thirteen independent projects. Water discharged from one section becomes the problem of the section downstream.

We analysed the network as a system, fixing the collection points and the discharge routes before sizing the pavement structures. The order matters: a correctly sized pavement placed on waterlogged ground breaks up within the first few seasons.

## DC 60, the link to Pănătău

The 13 roads include DC 60, the Cozieni – Punga – Pănătău commune road, on the section within Cozieni commune, from Pietrosu village (junction with county road DJ 203L) to the boundary with Pănătău commune. For this section, Cozieni obtained funding of about RON 11 million through the „Anghel Saligny” National Investment Programme. The 5.7 km section in Pănătău commune was funded separately, through the National Investment Company (CNI). Together, the two sections connect the area to national road DN10 in the Buzău Valley.

Source (in Romanian): [Opinia Buzău](https://opiniabuzau.ro/primaria-cozieni-poate-pierde-o-finantare-de-110-miliarde-lei/) (27 November 2024).

## Documentation produced

The project was prepared at the intervention approval documentation stage (DALI), as an intervention on existing roads.

## What it brings to the community

Cozieni sits in the Buzău Subcarpathians, where access depends directly on the condition of the local roads:

- A link with the outside world kept open all year round, including during periods of heavy rain
- Access for ambulances and fire crews in areas where, on a damaged road, a call-out could become impossible
- Transport of pupils to school, without interruptions caused by the state of the road
- Less isolation for the households on the outlying roads of the commune

## Improvements on site

The 13 roads, totalling 10.35 km, are treated as a network, not as isolated jobs. The drainage collection and discharge system is sized as a whole, with controlled discharge points. In hilly terrain this is the decisive element, because uncontrolled water erodes the slopes and washes material onto the carriageway.
