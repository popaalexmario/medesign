Protection of the slope above DN 7CC, the Călimănești Bypass, between km 5+750 and km 6+100, where material detached from the rock face was reaching the carriageway.

## Project context

The 350 m section runs through the gorge, between the rock face of the mountain to the east and the bank of the Olt River to the west. Rock detaching from the slope put traffic at risk.

The instability is driven by intense or prolonged rainfall and by repeated freeze-thaw cycles that weather the rock. The bedrock belongs to the crystalline Cozia Series (paragneiss, mica schist and quartz-feldspar gneiss), covered on the slopes by deluvial deposits with rock fragments. The site was classified in geotechnical category 2, moderate geotechnical risk.

## What we designed

We prepared the detailed design and provide technical assistance during construction, based on the solution set out in the site's structural assessment.

The works comprise scaling unstable rock blocks off the slope by hand, without explosives, then stabilising the surface with double-twisted wire mesh with corrosion protection, anchored with 6 m steel bars grouted with cement, spaced at most 2.40 m apart.

When the site was re-inspected in March 2026, the slope had deteriorated beyond the condition described in the original assessment: cracked blocks, new areas of shallow instability and erosion. The base solution remained valid but was adapted: high-strength mesh with a tensile strength of at least 230 kN/m, and a larger area of 3D erosion-control netting over the newly degraded zones.

## Status

The works are under way. Traffic runs in alternating single-lane operation, directed by flaggers.
