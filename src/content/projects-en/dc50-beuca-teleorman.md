Technical assistance during construction, for the roads discipline, on the rehabilitation of local road DC50 Beuca – Plopi – Zâmbreasca, damaged by floods, outside the built-up area of Beuca commune, Teleorman County.

## Project context

The works were carried out on three sections outside the village, 4,703 m in total: km 0+875 to km 2+012, km 2+380 to km 4+928 and km 5+413 to km 6+431. The road is technical class V.

The investment is delivered by the National Investment Company during construction, for Beuca commune, which owns it. The technical and economic indicators were approved by Order no. 4126/17.11.2020 of the Ministry of Development, Public Works and Administration.

## What we did

The detailed design was prepared by another designer. We provided technical assistance during construction: attending the critical inspection stages, analysing the situations that arose on site and setting out the solutions, issued as site instructions.

The road was rehabilitated with a 7.00 m formation, a 5.50 m carriageway and 0.75 m shoulders, on a semi-rigid pavement: 4 cm BA16 asphalt, 6 cm BAD22.4 binder, 20 cm stabilised base, 30 cm gravel and at least 20 cm capping layer. The works also included ditches, pipe culverts and side roads at property accesses.

During construction we issued two site instructions, based on site reports agreed by all parties:

- topping up the gravel sub-base to the design levels (3,495 m³) and excavation to level (1,242 m³), plus three additional side roads and nine additional D300 pipe culverts at property accesses
- concrete shoulders in C30/37 concrete, 50 cm wide and 10 cm thick, over 692 m, to keep the carriageway stable

All changes were included in the as-built documentation, in the construction's technical book.

## Status

The works are complete. In April 2026 we issued the specialist designer's statement for the final acceptance of the works.
