Design of access roads, parking and a port platform on the Borcea branch of the Danube.

## Project context

The tourist port development in Fetești Municipality covered the area along the Borcea branch of the Danube, combining road works with hydraulic engineering works.

## What the study covered

- **Internal roads** within the development
- **Visitor parking**
- **A port platform** on the bank of the Borcea branch

## Building on the Danube

Designing a port platform on a branch of the Danube is governed by the hydrological regime. The range between high and low water levels sets the platform levels, the bank protection solution and how the platform stays usable through the season.

On top of that come the specific approvals required for works in the riverbed and in the watercourse protection zone, which have a significant effect on the project schedule.

## Documentation phase

The project was prepared as a feasibility study (SF), the phase in which development scenarios are compared and the technical and economic indicators are established, before money is committed to the detailed technical design.

## What it brings to the community

A tourist port on the Borcea branch opens a line of development for Fetești:

- **Organised access to the Danube**, for residents and visitors
- **Local economic potential**: river tourism generates business for services in the town
- **Making use of a site** close to the town that had not been developed until now
- **Leisure infrastructure** in an area where the alternatives are limited

## Improvements on site

The study proposes roads and parking that make the area accessible and usable, together with the port platform on the bank of the Borcea branch. Platform levels are set against the range of Danube water levels, so that the development stays functional through the season and the bank is protected against erosion.
