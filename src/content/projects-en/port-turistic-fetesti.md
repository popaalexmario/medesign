Feasibility study for a tourist port on the Borcea branch of the Danube, in Fetești Municipality.

## Project context

The development covers the bank of the Borcea branch close to the town, a site that had not been developed until now. The port would give residents and visitors organised access to the Danube and a base for river tourism.

## What we designed

The feasibility study, prepared for Fetești Municipality, combines road works with hydraulic engineering works. It covered the internal roads, visitor parking and the port platform on the bank of the Borcea branch. Platform levels are set against the range of Danube water levels, so that the platform stays usable through the season and the bank is protected against erosion.

In the feasibility phase the development scenarios are compared and the technical and economic indicators are established, before the detailed technical design.
