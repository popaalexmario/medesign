The overbridge carrying DN7 (national road) over the main railway line at Gurasada, on the line upgraded for 160 km/h. We prepared the fabrication and execution details for the 70.00 m steel span.

## Project context

The overbridge is at railway km 506+217.83, corresponding to local km 0+623.27 on DN7, on Section 3: Gurasada–Simeria of the rehabilitation of the Frontieră–Curtici–Simeria line, part of Pan-European Corridor IV. The final client is the Romanian National Railway Company "CFR" S.A. The works on this section are carried out by the joint venture of FCC Construcción, Astaldi (now Webuild) and Contratas y Ventas.

The new overbridge takes the place of the old DN7 overbridge over the railway in the Gothatea area. It is 375 m long, with 14 spans of prestressed concrete beams and a 648-tonne central steel span over the main line.

## What we designed

We worked for the fabricator of the steel structure. We prepared the fabrication and execution details for the 70.00 m span, a through truss. At this stage the design becomes a working instruction for the workshop and the site. Over an electrified line, the clearance gauge includes the space for the overhead contact line, and the elements close to the catenary have protection measures against electric shock.

## Funding

Works contract no. 201 for Section 3 was signed on 30 August 2017, worth 2,636,523,184.68 lei excluding VAT. Payments come from the Cohesion Fund, through the Large Infrastructure Operational Programme 2014–2020, and from the state budget. Phase II of the project (SMIS code 327567) is also co-financed through the Transport Programme 2021–2027.

## Status

On Section 3, CFR opened track I of the new alignment between Ilia and Mintia to traffic on 11 November 2025, with three new bridges over the Mureș. The line is designed for 160 km/h for passenger trains and 120 km/h for freight trains.
