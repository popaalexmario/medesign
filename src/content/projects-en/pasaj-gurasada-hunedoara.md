Fabrication and execution details for the through-truss road overbridge, 70.00 m span.

## Project context

The road overbridge at railway km 506+217.83, corresponding to local km 0+623.27 on DN7 (national road), is part of **Section 3: Gurasada–Simeria**, a component of the rehabilitation of the Frontieră–Curtici–Simeria railway line, part of **Pan-European Corridor IV**.

The final beneficiary is the **Romanian National Railway Company "CFR" S.A.**, and the works on this section are carried out by the joint venture **FCC Construcción S.A. – Astaldi – Contratas y Ventas**. The documentation was prepared for the fabricator of the steel structure, within the project's execution chain.

## What is particular about overbridges over a railway

An overbridge crossing a railway line upgraded for speeds of up to 160 km/h is subject to requirements an ordinary road overpass does not have:

- **The clearance gauge** required by the electrified railway line, including the space for the overhead contact line
- **Protection against electric shock**, with specific measures on structural elements close to the catenary
- **Construction without interrupting rail traffic**, which drives the choice of structural solution and the staging
- **Coordination with the railway line design**, prepared by other teams

## Documentation prepared

We prepared the execution details, the phase at which the design becomes a working instruction for the workshop and the site. On a structure within a European corridor project, the documentation must also comply with the funder's requirements and the technical standards accepted under the financing agreement.

## What it brings to the community

The structure is part of the upgrade of a European rail corridor, with effects well beyond the site:

- **Higher line speeds** on the Frontieră–Curtici–Simeria line, up to 160 km/h
- **No more conflict between road and rail traffic**, through grade separation
- **Better safety** than a level crossing, where the risk of a serious accident is significant
- **Competitive rail transport**, helping shift freight from road to rail

## Improvements on site

The overbridge carries the road over the upgraded line, with the clearance gauge required by electrification and the protection measures for the overhead contact line. It was built without interrupting rail traffic, a condition that shaped both the structural solution and the staging of the works.
