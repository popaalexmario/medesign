A roundabout with two 5.50 m lanes at the southern entrance to Alba Iulia, on the city bypass (DN CALB), designed as a direct way in from the A10 motorway.

## Project background

The intersection sits in the Partoș district, where Regiment V Vânători Street and Ferdinand I Boulevard meet DN CALB, the city bypass that links to the Alba Iulia Sud junction of the A10 motorway.

In the existing layout there is no left turn from DN CALB onto the boulevard. Drivers coming off the motorway have to continue to the U-turn point at Liliacului Street, about 800 m away, or to the level crossing on Mărășești Street, about 3.5 km away, to get into the city. As a result, the Alba Iulia Sud junction was hardly used. On Regiment V Vânători Street, public transport crossed the path of vehicles moving from the middle lane towards DN CALB.

## What we designed

The at-grade, sign-controlled intersection becomes a roundabout that allows every movement between the boulevard, Regiment V Vânători Street and the bypass. The ring has an 11 m carriageway in two 5.50 m lanes, with an inner radius of 11 m and an outer radius of 22 m, sized for the swept paths of the heavy vehicles that transit the national road. The regulated speed in the roundabout is 30 km/h, and the permanently occupied area is 1,310 m².

Because the site is on a national road, the solution was coordinated with the standards for this road category and with the requirements of the road administrator.

## Funding

The investment is funded from the Alba Iulia municipal budget and other lawfully established sources. The total value in the technical and economic documentation is RON 6,121,691.13 including VAT, of which RON 4,424,400.26 is construction and installation work. The Local Council approved the project at its meeting of 20 December 2023, and the scheme was included in the city's 2024 budget for detailed design and construction.
