Roundabout on a national road with 2 traffic lanes of 5.50 m.

## Project background

The intersection of Regiment V Vânători Street, Ferdinand I Boulevard and the national road in Alba Iulia was studied for conversion into a roundabout.

## The solution studied

The roundabout was designed with **two traffic lanes of 5.50 m**, sized for the forecast traffic and for the swept paths of the heavy vehicles that transit the national road.

## Why a roundabout

At an at-grade intersection with significant traffic, a roundabout solves several problems at once:

- It reduces accident severity, by removing head-on conflict points and through the lower speed forced by the geometry
- It increases capacity compared with a signalised intersection, when traffic is balanced between the arms
- It removes the dependence on power supply and on the maintenance of traffic signal equipment

## Documentation produced

The project was prepared at the intervention approval documentation stage (DALI), as an intervention on an existing intersection. Because the site is on a national road, the solution had to be coordinated with the standards that apply to this road category and with the requirements of the road administrator.

## What it brings to the community

An intersection between a major urban artery and a national road is a point where both traffic and risk concentrate:

- Lower accident severity: the roundabout geometry removes head-on conflicts and forces lower speeds
- Smoother traffic at an intersection that serves urban and through traffic at the same time
- Predictability for drivers, through simple and uniform priority rules
- No dependence on power supply, unlike a signalised solution

## Improvements on site

The at-grade intersection with sign-controlled priority is replaced by a circular layout with two 5.50 m lanes, sized for the swept paths of the heavy vehicles that transit the national road. The layout clarifies the traffic movements between the boulevard, the adjacent street and the national road.
