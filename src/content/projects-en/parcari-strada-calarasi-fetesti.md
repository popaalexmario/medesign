Detailed design for the parking on Călărași Street, one of the main streets of Fetești, on the section between km 0+470 (junction with Bănățenilor Street) and km 0+960 (junction with Siretului Street).

## Project context

On this section, residents' cars were parked in the first lane of the street. One traffic lane was effectively lost.

## What we designed

We moved parking off the carriageway into dedicated bays beside the street, with spaces reserved for disabled drivers. The first lane went back to traffic.

Alongside the parking we rebuilt the pavements, the accesses to the apartment blocks and the green areas between them. At the junction with Ceahlău Street the pedestrian crossings are marked in red, and the vertical signage on the section includes pedestrian crossing, parking, children and 30 km/h speed limit signs.
