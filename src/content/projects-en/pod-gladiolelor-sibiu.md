Major repair of the bridge on Gladiolelor Street, known as the Maria Tereza Bridge, in the Terezian district of Sibiu.

## Project context

The bridge lies within the built-up area of Sibiu Municipality, at the end of Gladiolelor Street, between Cibinului Street and Râului Street. The area has historic value, so the solutions must be compatible with the cultural value of the structure and the documentation goes through additional approvals. The district is residential, and the bridge carries a link residents use every day.

## What we designed

We prepared the building permit design (DTAC) and detailed technical design (PTE) for Sibiu Municipality. The major repair works on the structural elements, based on the structural assessment that established the actual condition of the structure. Site set-up and traffic diversions were dealt with in the design itself, so that access for residents is disrupted as little as possible.
