The overpass carrying the Bucharest Ring Motorway (A0) over the Sun Motorway (A2), at km 53+069. We prepared the fabrication and execution details for the structure.

## Project context

The overpass is on Lot 1 of A0 South (km 52+070 to 69+000), the lot that connects the ring motorway to the A2. The final client is CNAIR, and the lot was designed and built by Alsim Alarko Sanayi Tesisleri ve Ticaret A.Ş.

## What we designed

We worked for MS Interproiect Engineering SRL, the fabricator of the steel structure. At the execution details (DE) stage the design becomes a direct instruction for the workshop and the site: fabrication details with geometry and tolerances, connection and erection details, and the construction sequence.

At an interchange between two motorways, clearances and load classes are higher than on the ordinary network, and the motorway being crossed stays open during the works. At this level of detail, a wrong dimension is not fixed on site. The element is made again.

## Funding

CNAIR signed the design-and-build contract for Lot 1 with Alsim Alarko in August 2019, worth about 830 million lei excluding VAT. At signing, the A0 project, part of the General Transport Master Plan, was planned for funding through the Large Infrastructure Operational Programme (POIM) 2014–2020.

## Status

The last 10 km of Lot 1 opened to traffic on 31 July 2024, together with the link between the A0 and the A2. Since then, southern Ilfov has had direct motorway access to the coast.
