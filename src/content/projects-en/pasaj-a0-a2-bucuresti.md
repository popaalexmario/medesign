Fabrication and execution details for the road overpass carrying the A0 over the A2 at Km 53+069.

## Project context

The overpass carrying the A0 Motorway over the A2 Motorway, at km 53+069, is part of the motorway ring around the Municipality of Bucharest. We prepared the **fabrication and execution details** for the overpass structure.

The final beneficiary is **CNAIR**, and the works on this section were carried out by the contractor **Alsim Alarko Sanayi Tesisleri ve Ticaret A.Ş.** The documentation was prepared for **MS Interproiect Engineering SRL**, the fabricator of the steel structure, within the project's execution chain.

## What the execution details (DE) phase means

Execution details are the level of documentation at which the design becomes a direct instruction for the workshop and the site. At this level the solution is no longer up for discussion. What matters is how exactly it gets built:

- **Fabrication details** for the elements made in the workshop, with geometry and tolerances
- Complete **reinforcement drawings**, with bar marks, diameters and bending schedules
- **Connection** and erection details
- The **construction sequence**

## What motorway projects demand

An interchange between two motorways carries stricter requirements than the ordinary road network: larger clearances, higher load classes and tight conditions for working under traffic, since the motorway being crossed stays open.

At this level of detail, a wrong dimension is not fixed on site. It is fixed by making the element again. The accuracy of the documentation has a direct cost consequence.

## What it brings to the community

The Bucharest motorway ring has effects across the whole region:

- **Diverting through traffic** away from the city's arterial roads, which affects congestion and air quality
- **Less heavy traffic** on the existing ring road, which runs through residential areas
- **A direct link between the major corridors**, without crossing the capital
- **Predictable journey times** for freight to and from the port of Constanța

## Improvements on site

The overpass at km 53+069 carries the A0 over the A2 motorway, with the clearances and load classes required for an interchange between two motorways. It was built while the motorway below remained in service, and that is what set the level of detail the documentation needed.
