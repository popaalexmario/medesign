Feasibility study, building permit design and detailed technical design for a multi-purpose sports pitch at Bădeni Secondary School, in Scobinți commune.

## Project context

The pitch is part of the project "Cross-border communities through education" (JeMS code ROMD00552), run by Scobinți commune. It serves the school's PE classes, mainly five-a-side football, and can host other sports as well.

## What we designed

The five-a-side pitch measures 31.50 x 17.50 m inside the playing lines, with a 1.50 m safety margin on each side. A technical area for eight benches runs along one long side, bringing the overall dimensions to 34.50 x 21.50 m. A footvolley court is marked on the same surface.

The playing surface is high-performance artificial turf designed for football. The pitch is enclosed by a 5 m high fence with two access gates.
