Reconfiguration of the DN1C junction with the Baia Mare bypass and a road overpass on DN1C with slip roads.

## Project context

The "Clubul Văcarilor" overpass, between Baia Mare and Recea, was studied for the Maramureș County Council, at the junction of DN1C (national road) with the Baia Mare bypass (E58).

## What the study covered

The solution analysed included:

- **Reconfiguring the junction** between DN1C and the bypass
- **The road overpass on DN1C**, to separate the traffic flows
- **The slip roads** providing the traffic movements between the two roads

## Junctions between national roads

When two national roads meet, an at-grade solution soon falls short: capacity is limited by the signal phases, and the risk of serious accidents grows with the design speed of both roads.

Grade separation solves capacity but brings a different problem: the land taken by slip roads and ramps. That is exactly what the feasibility study is for, comparing scenarios and finding the best solution given the land available, the cost and the forecast traffic.

## Documentation phase

The project was prepared as a feasibility study (SF), the phase in which alternative scenarios are analysed and the technical and economic indicators of the recommended option are established.

## What it brings to the community

The junction between DN1C and the Baia Mare bypass concentrates two kinds of traffic with different needs:

- **Separating through traffic from local traffic**, so that trips within the area are not blocked by the crossing flow
- **Shorter waiting times** at a point that was running at the limit of its capacity
- **Better safety**, by removing direct conflicts between flows at very different speeds
- **Conditions for the development of the Baia Mare – Recea area**, served by this link

## Improvements on site

The solution studied introduces grade separation through a road overpass on DN1C, with slip roads providing all the traffic movements between the two roads. The at-grade junction, limited by its waiting phases, is replaced by a layout in which the main flow no longer stops.

## Project status

The feasibility study we prepared formed the basis of an investment that has since been built and opened to traffic.

According to the official completion press release published by the Municipality of Baia Mare, the investment was implemented by **CNAIR (Romanian National Company for Road Infrastructure Administration) in partnership with Maramureș County and Baia Mare Municipality (local authorities)**, through **the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR), Component 4, Sustainable Transport**, under financing agreement no. 58 of 28.08.2024 with the Ministry of Transport and Infrastructure.

Key figures of the completed works:

- **122 m road overpass** on DN1C (E58), at the junction with the Baia Mare Bypass
- **Total length 443.6 m**, with two access ramps of 160.80 m
- **Roundabout built under the overpass**, at the junction of DN1C with the bypass
- **8 slip roads** distributing the traffic flows
- Associated infrastructure: street lighting, footways, road safety works and a traffic control and monitoring system
- **Total investment value: 99,463,913.34 lei**, of which 80,365,323.99 lei EU funds through PNRR
- **Accepted and opened to traffic on 17.07.2026**
