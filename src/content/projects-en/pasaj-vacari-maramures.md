The "Clubul Văcarilor" overpass, between Baia Mare and Recea, separates the traffic on DN1C (E58) from the traffic on the city's bypass. Our feasibility study formed the basis of the investment, opened to traffic in July 2026.

## Project context

The junction between DN1C (national road) and the Baia Mare bypass brings together through traffic on the E58 and local traffic towards Baia Mare and Recea. An at-grade junction between two national roads soon reaches its capacity, and conflicts between flows at different speeds raise the risk of serious accidents.

## What we designed

We prepared the feasibility study (SF) for the Maramureș County Council. The solution analysed included reconfiguring the junction between DN1C and the bypass, the road overpass on DN1C and the slip roads providing the traffic movements between the two roads. At the SF stage, scenarios are compared and the technical and economic indicators of the recommended option are established, against the land available, the cost and the forecast traffic.

## Funding

The investment was implemented by CNAIR (Romanian National Company for Road Infrastructure Administration) in partnership with Maramureș County and Baia Mare Municipality (local authorities), through the National Recovery and Resilience Plan (PNRR), Component 4, Sustainable Transport, under financing agreement no. 58 of 28.08.2024 with the Ministry of Transport and Infrastructure. The total value is 99,463,913.34 lei, of which 80,365,323.99 lei are EU funds through PNRR. Implementation period: 28.08.2024 to 31.08.2026.

## Status

The works were accepted and opened to traffic on 17.07.2026. They include:

- a 122 m road overpass on DN1C (E58), with a total length of 443.6 m including the two access ramps of 160.80 m
- a roundabout under the overpass, at the junction of DN1C with the bypass
- 8 slip roads
- street lighting, footways, road safety works and a traffic control and monitoring system
