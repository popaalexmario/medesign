Design for the upgrading and rehabilitation of 12.15 km of county road DJ 101S, with two bridges and embankment stabilisation, for Prahova County Council.

## Project background

DJ 101S starts in Comarnic, at the junction with national road DN1, and runs through Secăria towards the Doftana Valley. Along the route there is a succession of sections with different problems: stretches where the pavement structure is the main deficiency, stretches with an unstable embankment, and the two bridges.

## What we designed

The project was prepared at the intervention approval documentation stage (DALI), with the related technical expertise and site investigations, and treated the three categories of works as a whole: the pavement structure over 12.15 km, the two bridges and the embankment stabilisation. For the existing bridges, the assessment of their technical condition determined how the intervention was classed and which solutions were admissible. The bridge levels were tied into the road's longitudinal profile, and the stabilisation was matched to the loads from the final pavement structure.

## Funding

Prahova County Council approved the documentation and the technical and economic indicators at its meeting of 31 October 2023. The investment covers the section from km 0+000 (Comarnic, junction with DN1) to km 12+122, also includes energy-efficient street lighting, and has a total value of RON 75.5 million. According to the President of the County Council, the county roads approved at that meeting were to be funded through the new 2021–2027 Regional Operational Programme.
