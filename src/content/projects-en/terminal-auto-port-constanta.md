The car parking platform for the extension and upgrade of the PL6 Berth Car Terminal in the Port of Constanța.

## Project context

The car terminal at berth PL6 lies inside the Port of Constanța, managed by C.N. Administrația Porturilor Maritime S.A. Constanța. The extension called for a car parking platform that organises parking and keeps it apart from the terminal's operational traffic.

## What we designed

We prepared the building permit design (DTAC) and detailed technical design (PTE), with the details needed to build the platform. The platform is designed for the actual loads from vehicle manoeuvres and for the aggressive environment of the port. The grading design drains surface water across a large area with shallow falls. The design was coordinated with the port administration's operating regulations and access restrictions.
