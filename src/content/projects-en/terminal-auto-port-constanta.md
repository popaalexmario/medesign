Vertical alignment and grading design (sistematizare verticală), at the building permit (DTAC) and detailed design (PTE) stages, for the extension and upgrade of the car terminal at Berth PL6, Constanța Working Port.

## Project context

The car terminal at Berth PL6 lies within the Port of Constanța, in the Working Port area. The client is Romcargo Maritim S.A. The extension and upgrade of the terminal included the car parking platform, which organises vehicle storage and keeps it apart from operational traffic.

## What we designed

We prepared the vertical alignment and grading discipline at the DTAC and PTE stages: platform levels, falls and surface water drainage over a large area with shallow gradients, coordinated with the port's operating rules and access restrictions.
