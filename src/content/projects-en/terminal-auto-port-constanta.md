Design of a car parking platform.

## Project context

The extension and upgrade of the PL6 Berth Car Terminal in the Port of Constanța involved designing a car parking platform inside the port area.

## What is particular about port platforms

A platform in a car terminal is not designed like an ordinary car park. The governing factors are different:

- **Concentrated, repeated loads** from vehicle manoeuvres and handling equipment
- **Aggressive environment**: being close to the sea speeds up concrete deterioration and corrosion of steel elements
- **Surface water drainage** over large areas, with the shallow falls the platform's function requires
- **Continuity of operations**: the works are carried out without stopping the terminal

## Documentation prepared

We went through the building permit design (DTAC) and detailed technical design (PTE) phases: the documentation for the building permit and the detailed design, with the details needed to build the parking platform.

Projects inside a port area also require, compared with ordinary works, coordination with the port administration's operating regulations and its specific access restrictions.

## What it brings to the community

Port infrastructure has effects beyond the area where it sits:

- **More operating capacity** for the terminal, with an effect on cargo flows through the port of Constanța
- **Organised parking**, which stops uncontrolled occupation of the internal traffic lanes
- **Safe manoeuvring** on a platform designed for the vehicle types that actually use it
- **Shorter waiting times**, with an effect on logistics costs

## Improvements on site

The parking platform provides a surface designed for the actual loads and for the aggressive environment of the port. The grading and levelling design drains surface water across a large area with shallow falls, and the traffic layout separates parking from the terminal's operational traffic.
