Design for the upgrading of 6 local streets with a total length of 2.20 km.

## Project background

Aninoasa commune, in Dâmbovița County, started the upgrading of six local streets, 2.20 km in total. Rural local roads often reach the point where the existing pavement structure no longer matches today's traffic, and the stormwater collection system is incomplete or silted up.

## What the documentation covered

We prepared the full documentation, going through three successive stages for the same project:

- **DALI**, the intervention approval documentation, preceded by the structural assessment and the site investigations, establishing the optimal scenario and the technical and economic indicators
- **DTAC**, the building permit design (technical documentation for the construction permit)
- **PTE**, the detailed technical design, with discipline reports, specifications, bills of quantities and details

## Specific aspects

On a network of six streets treated as a whole, the factor that decides the service life of the investment is the continuity of drainage. Each section was analysed against the ground levels and the available outfalls, so that the solution works as a system, not as six separate jobs.

Classing the works as an intervention on existing construction, and not as a new project, was settled from the start. That decided the type of documentation and avoided having to repeat the procedure.

## What it brings to the community

Upgrading local streets changes concrete things in the daily life of a commune:

- Access for emergency services becomes predictable, including in bad weather, when an unsurfaced road can become impassable
- The school bus runs on a safe route, without detours forced by damaged stretches
- Costs for residents go down: vehicle wear on an upgraded road is significantly lower
- Dust disappears from the households along the road, with a direct effect on quality of life

## Improvements on site

On the six streets, the works bring a pavement structure sized for the actual traffic, a drainage collection and discharge system that protects the investment, and proper accesses to the properties. Correct connection to the existing network removes the spots where water ponded and gradually damaged the carriageway.
