Design for the upgrading of 6 local streets with a total length of 2.20 km, from intervention approval through to the detailed technical design.

## Project background

Aninoasa commune, in Dâmbovița County, started the upgrading of six local streets, 2.20 km in total. The works were classed from the start as an intervention on existing construction, not as a new project, and that settled the type of documentation.

## What we designed

We prepared the full documentation, in three successive stages for the same project:

- DALI, the intervention approval documentation, preceded by the structural assessment and the site investigations, with the optimal scenario and the technical and economic indicators
- DTAC, the building permit design
- PTE, the detailed technical design, with discipline reports, specifications, bills of quantities and details

On each street, the works include a pavement structure sized for the actual traffic, stormwater collection and discharge, and proper accesses to the properties. Each section was analysed against the ground levels and the available outfalls, so that drainage works continuously across the whole network, not as six separate jobs.

## Funding

The investment is funded through the „Anghel Saligny” National Investment Programme and appears on Aninoasa Town Hall's list of ongoing projects ([source, in Romanian](https://aninoasa.com/primaria-aninoasa/proiecte/proiecte-in-derulare/)).

## Status

The works have been carried out. The photos in the gallery show the streets after construction.
