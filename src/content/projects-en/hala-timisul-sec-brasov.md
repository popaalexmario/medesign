Site grading (sistematizare verticală) and the roads discipline, at the building permit (DTAC) and detailed design (PTE) stages, for a small production and storage hall with offices, fencing and utility connections on Timișul Sec Street in Brașov.

## Project context

The 6,088 m² plot lies on Timișul Sec Street, between the junctions with county road DJ103A and the DN VO1K bypass. A production and storage hall with offices is being built on the site for S.C. Power Trading S.R.L.

## What we designed

We were responsible for the site grading and the roads. We designed a rectangular paved platform of about 46 x 40 m for traffic and parking, connected to Timișul Sec Street by an access road about 50 m long.

The connection to the street includes a D600 pipe culvert, 9 m long, under the access, concrete-lined ditches of 25 m and 20 m tied into the existing ditch, and road signs at the exit. Surface water on the site is collected by a trafficable gutter and by soakaway ditches of 72 m and 130 m.
