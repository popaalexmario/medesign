The proposed project is within the built-up area, on a 5,200 m² plot in the public domain of Perșinari commune.

## Project background

The park in Perșinari commune, Dâmbovița County, is planned on a **5,200 m²** plot within the built-up area, in the public domain of the local authority.

## What a park involves, technically

A park looks like a simple job until it is designed properly. The components that decide whether it still works after a few years:

- Grading (vertical layout): the ground levels, so that water does not pond on the paths and in the activity areas
- Stormwater collection and discharge, sized for the entire area
- Path structure, sized for maintenance machinery, not only for pedestrians
- Accessibility for people with reduced mobility, with compliant slopes and transitions
- Utility networks for lighting and, where needed, irrigation

## The advantage of clear public ownership

Since the land is in the commune's public domain, the legal situation was clear from the start. In practice this is the issue that most often delays public investment projects, and here it was not an obstacle.

## Documentation produced

The project goes through the feasibility study (SF), which substantiates the technical and economic indicators, and the detailed technical design (PTE).

## What it brings to the community

For a commune, a landscaped park is often the first organised public space:

- A place for recreation for all ages, in a village where there are no alternatives
- A safe space for children, separated from road traffic
- A meeting point for the community, with a role in the social life of the village
- Use of a 5,200 m² public plot that was unused until now

## Improvements on site

The works introduce paths with a structure sized for maintenance machinery, grading that prevents water from ponding, stormwater collection over the whole area, and access for people with reduced mobility. These are the elements that make the difference between a park still usable in ten years and one that falls apart after the first few seasons.
