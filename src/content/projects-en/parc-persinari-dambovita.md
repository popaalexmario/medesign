A 5,200 m² park on a public plot within the built-up area of Perșinari commune, designed from the feasibility study through to the construction details.

## Project background

The land is in the public domain of Perșinari commune and lies within the built-up area. The legal situation was clear from the start, which allowed the work to go straight to design. For a commune, a landscaped park is often the first organised public space: a place for recreation for all ages and a safe space for children, separated from road traffic.

## What we designed

We prepared the feasibility study (SF), which substantiates the technical and economic indicators, and the detailed technical design (PTE).

The scheme includes grading, so that water does not pond on the paths and in the activity areas, stormwater collection over the whole area, and paths with a structure sized for maintenance machinery, not only for pedestrians. Slopes and transitions give access to people with reduced mobility. These are the elements that decide whether a park still looks good after ten years.
